Privacy Policy for VeloxFetch Browser Extension

VeloxFetch is designed with strict privacy and local-first principles. 

1. Data Collection:
The VeloxFetch browser extension interacts with your browser exclusively to detect download links, capture necessary authentication cookies/headers for protected downloads, and pass them securely to your local VeloxFetch desktop application. 

2. Data Storage and Transmission:
- We do NOT collect, store, or transmit any personal data, web history, or user activity to external servers, cloud databases, or third parties.
- All intercepted URLs, browser sessions, and download logs remain 100% local to your machine. 
- No telemetry or tracking scripts are used.

3. Changes to this Policy:
Any updates to this privacy policy will be reflected directly in our open-source repository.
